Evidence: `install.sh:5-7,125-128,183-224`, `scripts/sign-release.sh:60-116`, `.github/workflows/release.yml:10-11,134-141`.

Evidence: `install.sh:20,79-86,186-190,200-218`, `scripts/sign-release.sh:91,101`.
